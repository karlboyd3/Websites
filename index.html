Our Story

A Blacksburg
Tradition.

Carol Lee's Donuts has been a beloved part of the Blacksburg community for years. What started as a passion for perfect pastry has grown into a neighborhood institution — where Hokies and locals alike start their mornings right.

Every donut is made by hand, from scratch, using time-honored recipes. We don't cut corners, and we never freeze our dough. When we're sold out, we're sold out — so come early.
